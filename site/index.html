Clock lab (diagnostic, not a watch)

Replaces the watch firmware with a timekeeping test. The screen stays dark. Reinstall a watch build afterwards.

Current lab: v4, validation (version __VERSION__): a 15 min awake check, then 6 blocks of 2 h with 30 s sleeps and a 5 s wake every 10 min, each checked against network time, with temperature logged. About 12.5 hours. Every button below installs this same lab; A and B differ only in the sleep clock (A = the watch's current one).

Charge to full (on USB it shows "CHARGED - ready to unplug" in Logs & Console), save Wi-Fi from this page, unplug, and leave it in Wi-Fi range. When done it powers itself off: plug in USB, press PWR, open Logs & Console and save the log. Hold BOOT 5 s while on USB to erase results and start again.

First install

  1. Click your board's button and choose Install Electric Pocket Watch.
  2. When it finishes, the page asks for your Wi-Fi network and password. The watch joins once, sets its clock, and turns Wi-Fi off again.

Use a 2.4 GHz network. Choosing erase during install also erases the saved Wi-Fi and the battery log.

Change Wi-Fi later

On the watch: Settings → Wi-Fi → Choose network, pick one, type the password. Or from this page:

  1. Plug the watch in and click your board's button.
  2. Choose Change Wi-Fi (or Connect to Wi-Fi) and enter the network.

On the watch

  1. Buttons, plus touch in Settings: in the test builds touch works only inside the Settings menu. (The stable build still has touch everywhere and double-tap wake.)
  2. Wake: press BOOT or PWR.
  3. Change face: press BOOT.
  4. Settings: double-press BOOT. Touch the items, or press BOOT to move the highlight and hold it to select. Double-press to close. Wi-Fi (status, set time), Display (brightness, screen-on time, on while charging), System (battery, log, restart).
  5. Wi-Fi network: saved from this page over USB, right after installing.
  6. Set the time: hold BOOT for a second while the screen is on.
  7. Screen off: press PWR. Power off: hold PWR for 3 seconds; press PWR or plug in USB to turn it back on.

The 1.75 / 1.75-B and 2.06 have a battery-backed clock chip, so they keep the time while powered off. The 1.75C sets its clock over Wi-Fi after each power-on.
